Estate Heritage • Ceylon Trees (1959)

Tucked away in the beautiful hills of Kandy, The Botanist House is an intimate retreat secluded five minutes from the city centre.

The private terraces of the rooms and the pool offer beautiful views of the magnificent trees surrounding the property, many of which were planted in the 1940s by T. B. Worthington, the renowned botanist and author of Ceylon Trees (1959), who once called this very place home.

Today, his legacy lives on through the lush natural surroundings, giving the property a unique connection to Sri Lanka’s botanical heritage.

— T. B. Worthington, Botanist & Author
