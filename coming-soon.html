Estate Heritage • Ceylon Trees (1959)

An Intimate Botanical Retreat in the Kandy Hills

Tucked away in the beautiful hills of Kandy, The Botanist House is an intimate luxury retreat secluded five minutes from the city centre. The private terraces of the rooms and the pool offer breathtaking views of the magnificent trees surrounding the property.

Many of these specimens were planted in the 1940s by T. B. Worthington, the renowned botanist and author of Ceylon Trees (1959), who once called this very estate home. Today, his legacy lives on through the lush protected surroundings, offering guests a rare living connection to Sri Lanka’s botanical heritage.

“To live among ancient trees is to understand time itself.”
— T. B. Worthington, Ceylon Trees (1959)

Planted in the 1940s by botanist T. B. Worthington. A living botanical archive preserved across generations.
