The Story
Estate Heritage • Ceylon Trees (1959)

An Intimate Botanical Retreat in the Kandy Hills

Tucked away in the quiet hills of Kandy just five minutes from the city centre, The Botanist House is an intimate boutique sanctuary framed by magnificent, century-spanning canopies.

Many of these rare specimens were planted in the 1940s by T. B. Worthington, the renowned botanist and author of Ceylon Trees (1959), who once called this very estate home. Today, his living legacy endures as a tranquil botanical sanctuary preparing to welcome its first guests.

“To live among ancient trees is to understand time itself.” — T. B. Worthington, Ceylon Trees (1959)
